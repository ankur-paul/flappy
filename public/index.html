🐤 Flappy Feathers

Controls

  • Space / Click / Tap — flap
  • P or Esc — pause  ·  M — mute  ·  H — this panel
  • C — change your bird (or tap the bird on the title screen)
  • E — email your score from the game-over card

Surprises to find ✨

  • ⭐ Star — ghost mode: fly straight through pipes
  • 🧲 Magnet — coins come to you
  • 🍬 Candy — shrink down, tiny hitbox
  • ⏳ Hourglass — the world slows to a crawl
  • 🛡️ Bubble — survives one crash
  • 🪙 Coins — chain them fast for a ×5 combo
  • 🌟 Golden pipes are rare and worth 5 points
  • 🪶 A lucky feather drifts by now and then…
  • 😮 Squeeze past a pipe for a near-miss bonus
  • 🌗 Day turns to sunset, night and dawn as you fly
  • 🌧️ ❄️ Weather rolls in. 🎈 Balloons, 🛸 visitors and shooting stars drift past
  • 💨 Past 25 points the wind picks up, and pipes start to sway
  • 🏅 Earn bronze, silver, gold, platinum and rainbow medals
  • 🎨 New birds unlock at 10 / 20 / 35 / 50 points
